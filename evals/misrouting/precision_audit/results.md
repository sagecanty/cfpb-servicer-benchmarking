# Blind Precision Audit — Results

Adjudicated 60 cases blind (verdicts hidden during labeling).

## Flag precision (certifies the 55.8%)

Of 35 probe-flagged complaints, human agreed **35 are real misroutes** = **100% precision** (95% CI 90–100%).

Reading: the census flagged 55.8% of the category as misrouted; at 100% precision, roughly 56 percentage points of that 55.8% are human-confirmed misroutes — the finding holds directionally at census scale.

## Did the probes miss real misroutes?

In the 15 'belongs' cases sampled, human found 3 that are actually misroutes (probe false-negatives).

Overall flag recall on this sample: 76% (35/46 human-called misroutes were probe-flagged).

## One-probe uncertainty band

Of 10 one-probe cases, 8 are true misroutes (80%) — leans misroute, so the true rate sits between the strict 55.8% and the 66.9% upper band.

## Scope

- Single adjudicator (the author), 60-case blind sample. A second independent labeler would let us report inter-rater agreement; this is one honest pass, not a multi-rater gold standard.
